When a Loved One Passes

We know this is hard. Take one step at a time. You are not alone. This guide is here to help you through the first days with clear steps, your rights, and real local help.

Created with love by Edwin & Al Roman, inspired by our beloved Mother, born 1937 • The Roman Family • ZIP 07030

1. Immediate Checklist - First 24-48 Hours

📞 Call for Help

If a death is unexpected or there is an immediate emergency, call 911. If the person is under hospice care, call the hospice nurse first. For an expected death outside hospice, follow the instructions given by the doctor or local authorities.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Call Family & Clergy

Let close family and spiritual support know. You don't have to carry this alone. Ask someone to be with you.

⏸️ Pause - Don't Rush Belongings

Do NOT rush to clean out belongings. There is no deadline. Give yourself days or weeks. It's okay to wait.

📄 Locate Important Papers

Will, life insurance policies, Social Security card, birth certificate, marriage certificate, DD214 if veteran, bank and mortgage info.

⚖️ Funeral Home - You Can Compare

You have a LEGAL right to call multiple homes and compare. Ask for General Price List (GPL). No rush to decide. Get prices over phone.

🏛️ Notify Social Security & Others

Call 1-800-772-1213. Also notify pension, VA if veteran, employer. Funeral home often helps with this.

🏠 Secure Home, Pets, Mail

Lock home, arrange pet care, hold mail at USPS.com, notify close neighbors, secure valuables.

💛 Grief Support is Okay

If grief becomes a mental health crisis or you feel unsafe, call or text 988 for 24/7 crisis support. Veterans can press 1 for the Veterans Crisis Line.

🛡️ Your Rights Under FTC Funeral Rule - Know Before You Go:
  • You have right to itemized General Price List (GPL) - by law they must give it when you visit in person, and must give prices over phone
  • You can buy casket from third-party (Costco, Amazon, local casket store) and funeral home MUST accept it with NO extra fee - median metal casket $2,500 but can be much less elsewhere
  • You can decline embalming - except special cases, it's not required by law
  • You can decline anything you don't want - only basic services fee is non-declinable (non-declinable). Everything else optional
  • Online pricing: Federal law does not currently require funeral homes to post the General Price List online. Many do so voluntarily. You can ask for prices by phone and must receive the GPL when you inquire in person.
  • Compare options: Burial, cremation, and other lawful options vary by location, tradition, and budget. Ask providers for current local prices before deciding.

3. Choosing: Burial vs Cremation vs Green Options

⚰️
Traditional Burial

Viewing, funeral ceremony, casket, burial at cemetery. Most familiar. Requires cemetery plot, vault, headstone extra costs.

Best if: Family tradition, cemetery plot already owned
🔥
Direct Cremation

Simplest, most affordable. No viewing or ceremony at funeral home. Includes care, paperwork, alternative container, cremation, return of remains. You can hold memorial later anywhere - home, park, church.

Most affordable • $1,995 median
🕊️
Cremation with Memorial

Cremation plus a memorial service with urn present. Flexible timing, lower cost than burial. Family can plan when ready.

Popular middle option
🌿
Green / Natural Burial

No embalming, biodegradable casket or shroud, natural setting. $2,000-$5,000. Growing option, environmentally gentle.

Eco-friendly
💧
Water Cremation (Aquamation)

Alkaline hydrolysis - gentle water process. Legal in majority of US states as of 2026. Ask local providers if offered.

Legal in most states
🇺🇸
Veterans Benefits

FREE burial at national cemetery, FREE marker, flag, Presidential Memorial Certificate. VA also offers burial allowance.

VA: 1-800-827-1000

5. Financial Help & Grief Support

💰 Financial Assistance - What Can Help
  • •Social Security: $255 one-time death benefit + monthly survivor benefits for spouse/children - Call 1-800-772-1213
  • •Veterans: VA burial benefits and allowance amounts vary by eligibility and circumstances. Confirm current benefits directly with VA - 1-800-827-1000
  • •County DSS: Many counties help low-income families with burial/cremation - call your county Department of Social Services
  • •Life Insurance: Check policies, employer group life, credit union accidental death, auto insurance
  • •211.org: Dial 211 for local assistance programs - they know local charities that help
  • •Crowdfunding & Church: Many families get help from faith community, GoFundMe
💛 Grief Support - You Are Not Alone
  • •988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline: Call or text 988 - 24/7 grief support, press 1 for Veterans Crisis Line
  • •Eldercare Locator: 1-800-677-1116 - connects to local bereavement groups for seniors
  • •Hospice Bereavement: Even if your loved one was not on hospice, local hospices offer FREE grief groups open to community
  • •Faith & Community: Clergy, church, temple, mosque - don't isolate
  • •Important Tips: Don't pay full amount upfront before receiving GPL. Don't feel pressured into most expensive casket. It's okay to say "I need time to compare." Take a trusted friend with you.
