Sponsorship & Affiliate Disclosure

No hidden paid recommendations.

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Current status

Senior Care Compass currently presents itself as free, with no advertising. The current resource links and provider results are not displayed because Senior Care Compass receives a referral fee, affiliate commission, or paid placement from the listed provider or public program.

If monetization is added later

If a future provider, advertiser, affiliate network, sponsor, or other business pays for placement, referrals, leads, or promotion, the relationship should be disclosed clearly and conspicuously near the relevant recommendation or link—not hidden only in this policy.

Sponsored content must stay distinguishable

Paid material should be labeled so an ordinary visitor can tell it is advertising or sponsored content. Compensation must not change factual safety information, emergency guidance, eligibility rules, or the presentation of public provider data in a misleading way.

Testimonials and reviews

Senior Care Compass should not present invented people as genuine customers or caregivers. If real testimonials are added, they should reflect actual experiences, be used with permission, and include any material connection or incentive that could affect how a visitor evaluates the statement.

Advertising reference: FTC endorsement guidance says unexpected material connections that could affect how consumers evaluate an endorsement should be disclosed clearly and conspicuously.
