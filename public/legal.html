Legal & Privacy Center

Clear protections for visitors and for Senior Care Compass.

These pages explain how the site works, its limits, how public data is used, what content is protected, and what changes would require additional disclosures.

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Current operating model: Senior Care Compass is a free informational website with no login, no advertising, no payment processing, and no user health-record storage. The ZIP finder sends a ZIP code through our server-side lookup to public data services so local provider results can be displayed.
