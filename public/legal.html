Clear protections for visitors and for Senior Care Compass.

These pages explain how the site works, its limits, how public data is used, what content is protected, and what changes would require additional disclosures.

Last updated: November 27, 2026

Current operating model: Senior Care Compass is a free informational website with no login, clearly labeled optional commercial promotions, no on-site payment processing, and no user health-record storage. Roman Legacy Publishing publications are sold on Gumroad, subject to its checkout terms and privacy policy. Purchases are not required to access free care resources. The ZIP finder sends a ZIP code through our server-side lookup to public data services so local provider results can be displayed.
