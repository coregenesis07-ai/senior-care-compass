Third-Party Data & External Links

Useful data still needs verification.

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Public/provider data

The live ZIP finder may use CMS Provider Data, the NPPES NPI Registry, and a ZIP-to-location service. Results are transformed for easier reading but remain dependent on the source data and matching logic available at the time of the request.

Exact ZIP versus nearby care

The current finder is designed to return exact-ZIP matches for supported provider categories. A provider just outside the ZIP may be closer than one inside it, and some categories may return no exact-ZIP result. Use Medicare Care Compare, HRSA, 211, Eldercare Locator, health-plan directories, or other official/local resources to broaden a search.

NPI limitations

An NPI identifies a health-care provider record. It does not, by itself, prove current licensure, credentials, quality, public availability, Medicare participation, or fitness for a particular patient. Verify those matters independently.

Ratings and quality information

If the site displays a CMS rating, it is presented as source data—not as Senior Care Compass’s own rating or recommendation. The source may revise methodology or values over time.

External destinations

Outside sites may change URLs, eligibility rules, content, accessibility, cookies, privacy policies, or security practices without notice to Senior Care Compass. A link does not imply sponsorship, endorsement, partnership, or government affiliation unless expressly stated.
